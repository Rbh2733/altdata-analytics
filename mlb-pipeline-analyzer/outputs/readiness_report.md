## Disclosures

1. Production only. Defense, park effects, and baserunning beyond
   steals are excluded, so the score systematically flatters bat-first
   prospects and understates glove-first ones.
2. The age credit is the dominant source of generosity, measured above,
   not the translation factors. It is a predictive-weight equivalence
   (Stoltz 2022), larger than what open-source implementations use, and
   it ships as its own severable column everywhere.
3. Translation factors are 2016-vintage Davenport estimates applied as
   level tiers. Triple-A's current inflated run environment makes AAA
   readings generous, visible in the calibration table.
4. Dominican Summer League stints are excluded from scoring. No cited
   translation factor covers the DSL, and the rookie-level age baseline
   pools it with US complex ball, which let DSL teenagers max the age
   cap against leagues they never played in.
5. The positional adjustment uses the position on the player's earliest
   ranking-list row (pre-debut vintage), because the API's position
   label is current-day even in historical payloads. The cost is coarse
   buckets (INF, OF) with in-house averaged constants.
6. The player's own inputs are strictly pre-debut. League baselines and
   level average ages are full-season aggregates, per the season-totals
   design ruling, so debut-day readings use season-level league context
   that finished forming after the debut. Disclosed, not hidden.
7. wOBA weights are one MLB season's constants (2023, scale 1.204)
   applied across all levels and seasons.
8. The pitcher replacement offset per 600 batters faced is an in-house
   symmetry choice, not a published convention.
